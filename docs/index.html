Secure Local Storage

Your contacts belong to you. ScanDex stores everything locally using Dexie.js on top of IndexedDB. It handles complex relational data between Events and Contacts with ease.

  • No cloud synchronization required
  • Instant load times
  • Stores high-res card images offline

Voice Notes & Full Export

Record multiple voice notes for each contact right from the app. When you're ready, generate a comprehensive ZIP Archive containing your CSV spreadsheet alongside all raw ID card images and audio files.

  • Infinite voice notes per contact
  • Offline ZIP generation
  • Avoids email limits with Native Android Sharing
